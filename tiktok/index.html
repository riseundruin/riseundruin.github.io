Rise and Ruin Publisher

This private tool sends the Rise & Ruin channel's own short videos to our TikTok account's inbox as drafts. Nothing is posted automatically: the account owner reviews the caption, privacy and interaction settings in the TikTok app and posts each video himself.

What the tool will be allowed to do

  • user.info.basic: read your TikTok display name, avatar and open ID, to confirm the right account is connected.
  • video.upload: upload our videos to your TikTok inbox as drafts for you to edit and post.

Connect TikTok

You can remove access any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services. See our Privacy Policy and Terms.
