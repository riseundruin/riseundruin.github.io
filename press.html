About Rise & Ruin

Rise & Ruin is an independent YouTube channel of short, sourced documentaries about how business empires rise and why they fall. Each episode, called an Empire File, tells the story of one company, from Nokia and Blockbuster to Enron, Lehman Brothers and Pan Am, and ends with three lessons for founders, investors and anyone who works inside a big company. Rise & Ruin was founded in 2026 by M J.
