Hollywood Spent $1.75 Billion on an App Nobody Wanted

The story

In April 2020, Quibi launched with $1.75 billion in funding, two of America's most famous executives, Hollywood's biggest stars, and a Super Bowl ad. Six months later, it announced it was shutting down.

This is the story of how Jeffrey Katzenberg and Meg Whitman built a premium streaming app for your phone, why only a small fraction of early users paid after the free trial, and what every business owner can learn about testing demand before spending big.

Three lessons

  1. 1

    Money cannot replace demand. Quibi had every resource except proof that people wanted it.

  2. 2

    Test small before you spend big. A cheap experiment could have revealed the problem before billions were spent.

  3. 3

    Don't fight how customers already behave. People share, screenshot, and watch on any screen. Quibi made them stop.
