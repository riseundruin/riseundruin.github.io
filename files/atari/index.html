Why Did Atari Fail? The Company That Buried Its Games in the Desert

Atari1983: The video game crashHype over product4 min watch
▶  Watch on YouTubeSubscribe for the next file

The story

Why did Atari fail? In September 1983, trucks dumped Atari game cartridges in a New Mexico landfill and workers poured concrete over them. One year earlier, Atari was bringing in about $2 billion a year.

This is the story of Atari: the $500 start, Pong, the quarters that jammed the first machine, the Atari 2600, the programmers who walked out and founded Activision, twelve million Pac-Man cartridges, the E.T. game built in about five weeks, the crash of 1983, and the Alamogordo dig in 2014.

Three lessons

  1. 1

    Your talent is your product. Atari let its best programmers walk, and they built a rival.

  2. 2

    Hype is not demand. Twelve million Pac-Man cartridges, for about seven million buyers.

  3. 3

    Protect your quality. When stores filled with bad games, shoppers stopped trusting all of them.
