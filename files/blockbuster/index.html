Blockbuster Almost Beat Netflix. Here's What Really Killed It.

The story

In 2000, Netflix offered to sell itself to Blockbuster for $50 million, and Blockbuster said no. That's the famous version. The real story is stranger: a few years later, Blockbuster was actually beating Netflix. Then it walked away from the plan that was working.

This is the rise and fall of the blue and yellow giant, from a single Dallas store in 1985 to the last Blockbuster on Earth.

Three lessons

  1. 1

    The famous mistake is not always the fatal one. Saying no to Netflix hurt; abandoning a strategy that was working hurt more.

  2. 2

    Debt takes away your options. You can't win a long war with a short runway.

  3. 3

    Never build your profits on your customers' pain. The moment they find a better option, they will leave.
