The story

In 2005, three investment firms bought Toys R Us for $6.6 billion. More than $5 billion of it was borrowed, and the toy store itself had to pay it back. Twelve years later, Toys R Us was bankrupt. Six months after that, all 735 of its American stores were closing.

This is the story of the toy store every child knew: the backwards R, the ten-year Amazon deal that went to court, the leveraged buyout, and the brand that refused to die.
