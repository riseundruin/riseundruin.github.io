How Nokia Lost 40% of the Phone Market

Nokia2013: Sells its phone business to MicrosoftMissed the shift7 min watch
▶  Watch on YouTubeSubscribe for the next file

The story

In 2007, almost 4 in every 10 phones sold worldwide were Nokias. Less than seven years later, Nokia sold its entire phone business to Microsoft.

This is the story of how a Finnish paper mill became the king of mobile phones, why a company with every advantage couldn't answer the iPhone, and the surprising way Nokia survived.

Three lessons

  1. 1

    Being number one is not a strategy. Nokia's size made it slow, not safe.

  2. 2

    Fear kills good information. If your team is afraid to bring you bad news, you will only hear it when it is too late.

  3. 3

    The market doesn't reward the best hardware. It rewards the best ecosystem, and the best experience.
