Lehman Brothers Survived 158 Years. It Didn't Survive One Weekend.

The story

On September 15, 2008, Lehman Brothers filed for bankruptcy with $639 billion in assets, the biggest bankruptcy in American history. The firm had lived through the Civil War and the Great Depression. It didn't survive one weekend in 2008.

This is the story of a small Alabama store that became a giant of Wall Street: the cotton trade, the housing bet, borrowing at 30 to 1, the Repo 105 accounting trick, and the weekend nobody came to the rescue.

Three lessons

  1. 1

    Borrowing makes everything bigger, the wins and the losses. At thirty to one, there is no room for mistakes.

  2. 2

    If you have to hide a number, the number is the problem.

  3. 3

    History is not protection. A hundred and fifty eight years of reputation disappeared in one weekend.
