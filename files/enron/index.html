Enron Was America's Most Innovative Company. Its Profits Were Invented.

The story

In August 2000, Enron's stock hit $90. Fifteen months later it was worth 61 cents. Fortune had called it the most innovative large company in America. What it was really inventing was its profits.

This is the story of how a gas pipeline company became a Wall Street star: mark to market accounting, the Blockbuster streaming deal, the hidden side companies, the warning letter, and the biggest bankruptcy in American history at the time.

Three lessons

  1. 1

    Profit you haven't earned yet isn't profit. Cash is what's real.

  2. 2

    If nobody can explain how you make money, that is a warning, not a compliment.

  3. 3

    Listen to the person who raises the alarm. Enron had one, and she was right.
