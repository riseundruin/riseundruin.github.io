BlackBerry Saw the iPhone Coming. Here's Why It Still Lost.

BlackBerry2016: Stops designing its own phonesMissed the shift6 min watch
▶  Watch on YouTubeSubscribe for the next file

The story

In 2009, the President of the United States fought to keep his BlackBerry. Less than five years later, BlackBerry lost nearly a billion dollars in a single quarter and cut 40% of its workforce.

BlackBerry's founders weren't blind to the iPhone. One of them watched it launch and asked, "How did they do that?" This is the story of how the phone the world couldn't put down got put down, and the surprising way the company survived.

Three lessons

  1. 1

    Your best customers can blind you. Business users loved keyboards. Everyone else moved on.

  2. 2

    Success can hide a crisis. When sales are still growing, it is easy to believe the danger isn't real.

  3. 3

    Know your true strength. BlackBerry's real edge was trust and security, and that is what saved it.
