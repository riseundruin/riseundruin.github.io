Why Did Pan Am Fail? The Airline That Took Reservations for the Moon

Pan Am1991: Stops flyingDebt and overreach5 min watch

The story

Why did Pan Am fail? More than 93,000 people signed up for Pan Am's first flights to the Moon. Twenty years later, the airline that defined the jet age was gone.

This is the story of Pan Am: the Key West mail route, the Clipper flying boats, the first American jet across the Atlantic, the Boeing 747 bet, the First Moon Flights Club, the National Airlines merger, the sale of its crown jewels, and the end in December 1991.

Three lessons

  1. 1

    Build your base before you go global. Pan Am conquered the world, but had no home market to fall back on.

  2. 2

    Selling your best assets to pay the bills only buys time. Once they're gone, they're gone.

  3. 3

    A famous brand is not a business plan. Everyone knew Pan Am. Not enough people flew it.
