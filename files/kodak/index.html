Kodak Invented the Digital Camera. So Why Did It Go Bankrupt?

Kodak2012: Files for bankruptcyMissed the shift7 min watch

The story

In 1975, a young Kodak engineer built the world's first handheld digital camera. Management's reaction, as he remembers it: "That's cute, but don't tell anyone about it." Thirty six years later, Kodak filed for bankruptcy.

But Kodak didn't simply ignore digital. By 2005, it was selling more digital cameras in America than anyone else. This is the story of how the company that invented the future was destroyed by it, and how its biggest rival, Fujifilm, survived the same storm.
