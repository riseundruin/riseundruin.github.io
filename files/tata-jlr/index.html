Tata Tried to Sell to Ford. 9 Years Later, It Bought Jaguar.

Tata JLR2008: Buys Jaguar Land Rover from FordThe comeback9 min watch
▶  Watch on YouTubeSubscribe for the next file

The story

In 1999, Tata flew to Detroit to sell its struggling car business to Ford, and according to a Tata executive who was there, the meeting was humiliating. Nine years later, Ford was the one selling, and Tata bought Jaguar and Land Rover for $2.3 billion. Then the financial crisis hit.

This is the story of how an Indian company, famous for trucks and the world's cheapest car, turned two struggling British brands into a business earning billions, and why the story still isn't over.

Three lessons

  1. 1

    Buying cheap only works if you can survive what comes next. Tata bought at a great price, then nearly drowned in the crisis.

  2. 2

    Own it, don't overwrite it. Tata let the British brands stay British, and invested in their engineers and designers.

  3. 3

    Keep investing through the storm. The cars that saved the company were paid for in the years it was losing money.
