How empires rise. Why they fall.

Rise & Ruin is a YouTube channel of short documentaries, called Empire Files, about companies that climbed to the top and then broke: Nokia, Blockbuster, Kodak, Enron, Lehman Brothers, Pan Am, Atari and more. Every file is researched from published sources and ends with the lessons behind the fall.
